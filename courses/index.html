The Stanford courses I recommend most

我在 Stanford 最推荐的两门课

Two courses shaped how I work more than any others. CS197 taught me how to do research — how to ask a question, run a fair test, and write up what actually happened. CS329Z taught me how to build — how to engineer an AI agent, and how to tell whether it deserves to be trusted. Each course, in one mind map:

这两门课对我影响最大：CS197 教我怎么做研究——怎么提问题、怎么做公平的实验、怎么诚实地写下结果；CS329Z 教我怎么做东西——怎么构建 AI 智能体，又怎么判断它到底值不值得信任。下面每门课一张思维导图。

CS197 · Computer Science Research

CS197 · 计算机科学研究入门

In CS197 you do not study research from the outside — you run one. Our team asked whether the way you prepare numbers before feeding them to a model changes what the model learns, and the honest answer surprised us: the plainest preparation was the best overall, one fancy method was best at exactly one thing and worst at everything else, and a popular trick failed completely. Learning to report that — surprises included — is the real curriculum.

在 CS197，你不是在课外看别人做研究，而是自己真正跑一个项目。我们组的问题是：把数字喂给模型之前先怎么处理，会不会改变模型学到的东西？诚实的答案出乎意料：最朴素的处理整体最好；一个花哨的方法只赢了一项、其他全输；一个流行技巧则彻底失败。学会把这样的结果——包括意外——如实写出来，才是这门课真正的教学内容。

CS329Z · Engineering AI Agents

CS329Z · AI 智能体工程

CS329Z is not a course about prompting. You write the retrieval, the tool calls, and the agent loop yourself before any framework is allowed, and then you spend the second half of the course on the harder question: how do you know it works? My final project, Medical MythTriage, came straight out of that question — and its main finding is the course in one line: a fast model’s confidence tracks how fluent a claim sounds, not whether it is true.

CS329Z 不是一门讲提示词的课。框架不许先用——检索、工具调用、智能体循环都要先自己写出来；然后用后半学期回答更难的问题：你怎么知道它真的管用？我的期末项目 Medical MythTriage 正是从这个问题里长出来的，它的核心结论就是这门课的一句话版本：快速模型的自信，跟着一句话听起来有多顺走，而不是跟着它是不是真的走。

If you are a student choosing courses at Stanford and you can only take two of mine, take these — one for the research, one for the building. 如果你在 Stanford 选课，只能选两门我上过的，就选这两门——一门为了研究，一门为了动手做东西。
