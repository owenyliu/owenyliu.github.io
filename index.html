About

I am a Master's student at Stanford University (Clinical Informatics Management, School of Medicine) and a researcher with the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (SAIL).

I work on data and evals for AI agents. I am a contributor to Terminal-Bench and OpenThoughts.

Before Stanford, I co-founded TATA Technology in Hong Kong, a marketplace that matched 20,000+ families of autistic children with trained providers; it was acquired after four years. Earlier, I was a director at J.P. Morgan. I read mathematics at Baruch College (B.S., top 1%).
